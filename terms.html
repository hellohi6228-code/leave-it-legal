What Leave it does

Leave it helps the people involved in a property transaction agree on which items stay with the property. An agent creates a workspace for an address and shares invite codes. The seller or landlord photographs items. The buyer or renter marks each item as wanted or not wanted.

Leave it records preferences, not agreements

Nothing recorded in the app forms a contract between any parties. Marking an item "Leave it!" or "I'm OK" records a preference for discussion. It creates no obligation on any party to leave, remove, transfer, buy or sell anything, and it does not amend any purchase agreement, lease, listing agreement or other document. Any binding arrangement about fixtures, fittings or chattels must be made in the transaction documents themselves.

Leave it is not a party to your transaction. We provide no legal, financial, valuation or real estate advice.
